1. Who we are

Basaer Media operates the Instagram and Facebook page @basaer.media.bh, which shares Quran verses, recitations and Islamic reminders in Arabic and English. The "Basaer Publisher" app is a private tool used only by the page owner to schedule and publish this content.

2. What data the app accesses

The app connects only to the Basaer Media Instagram account that its owner authorized. It uses an access token to publish posts and Reels to that account. It does not collect, read or store messages, comments, followers or any personal information of visitors, followers or other users.

3. What we store

Only the access token and the account identifier of the page owner, kept in the owner's private automation system. Published content (images, videos, captions) is stored temporarily on a media hosting service and deleted after publishing.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone. Content is published through Meta's official APIs and is subject to Meta's own policies.

5. Retention and security

Tokens are stored in access-restricted systems and are replaced or deleted when no longer needed.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

7. Your choices and data deletion

Because the app does not collect personal data from visitors, there is nothing to delete for them. If you believe we hold any data about you, email multiapps86@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

The page owner can also remove the app at any time in Instagram settings (Apps and Websites) or Facebook settings (Business Integrations), which revokes its access.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above.
